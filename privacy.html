Privacy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Unique for Logistics LLC (“we”) publishes this site to describe Cycle TMS, a transportation management system for motor carriers, and to receive requests for a review.

This website

The review form does not store a message on this site. It opens an email to info@cycletiger.com on your device. If you send that email, we use your name, company, fleet size, and note to answer you.

We do not sell personal information. We do not run advertising trackers on this page.

The Cycle system

When a carrier uses Cycle TMS, operational data stays in that carrier’s system. That data can include owners, trucks, drivers, brokers, orders, trip status, bills of lading, proof of delivery, location on the order, invoices, and payment status.

A driver application, if the carrier adds one later, reads and updates the same order. It is not part of the base system.

A carrier agreement covers retention, who may see an order, and how a carrier can ask for export or deletion of its data. This page is not that agreement.
